For some complex-valued function $u$ defined over some $n$-dimensional Euclidean domain $\Omega$, we define the Fourier transform of $u$ as 
$$(\mathcal F u)(\xi)=\hat u(\xi)=\int_\Omega e^{-ix\cdot\xi}u(x)dx$$
where $\xi$ is a frequency covector, that is $\xi:\Omega\to\R$. We say $\xi\in\Omega^*$, the dual space to $\Omega$.

The inverse Fourier transform of $\hat u$ is defined as
$$(\mathcal F^{-1} \hat u)(\xi)=u(x)=(2\pi)^{-n}\int_{\Omega^*} e^{ix\cdot\xi}\hat u(\xi)d\xi$$
where $x$ is a position vector, that is $x:\Omega^*\to \R$.
