The triangle inequality for $L^p$ spaces.

Let $\Omega$ be a measure space and $f,g\in L^p(\Omega)$ and $p\in[1,\infty)$. Then
$$\|f+g\|_p\leq \|f\|_p+\|g\|_p,$$
with equality holding if and only if $f$ and $g$ are positively linearly dependent.

If $0<p<1$, the reverse inequality holds:
$$\|f\|_p+\|g\|_p\leq\|f+g\|_p.$$

The proof follows from [[Hölder´s inequality]]
