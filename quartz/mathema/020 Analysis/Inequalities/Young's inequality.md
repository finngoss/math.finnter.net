If $a,b\in\R_{\geq0}$ and $p,q\in\R_{>1}$ such that $\frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}=1$ then
$$ab\leq \frac{a^p}{p}+\frac{b^q}{q},$$
with equality holding if and only if $a^p=b^q$.

Young's inequality often appears in the "rob peter to pay paul" format:

For $\epsilon>0$ we have
$$ab\leq\frac{a^2}{2\epsilon}+\frac{\epsilon b^2}{2}$$
